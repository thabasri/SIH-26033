## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1.Develop a digital marketplace connecting farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
2.Allow farmers to register and upload their products, quantities and prices.
3.Enable buyers to compare prices and purchase products directly.
4.Eliminate unnecessary intermediaries in the supply chain.
5.Provide transparent pricing to ensure fair earnings for farmers.
6.Arrange transportation through integrated logistics support.
7.Use AI to predict market demand and help farmers plan production.

## Technical Approach
1. Develop a user-friendly web and mobile application.
2. Use HTML, CSS and JavaScript for frontend development.
3. Use Python and Flask for backend development.
4. Use MySQL for database management.
5. Implement Machine Learning for demand forecasting.
6. Integrate Google Maps API for route optimization.
7. Provide farmer and buyer registration with secure login.
8. Implement product listing, ordering and payment features.

## Feasibility and Viability

1. Uses easily available technologies.
2. Can be accessed through smartphones.
3. Supports small-scale farmers and FPOs.
4. Can integrate with existing transportation services.
Challenges:
1. Limited internet connectivity in rural areas.
2. Lack of digital awareness among farmers.
3. Transportation and storage difficulties.
4. Maintaining product quality during delivery.
Solutions:
1. Provide regional language support.
2. Conduct digital awareness programmes.
3. Partner with local transport providers.
4. Introduce quality verification systems.
## Impact and Benefits
1. Increases farmers' earnings by reducing intermediaries.
2. Provides affordable agricultural products to consumers.
3. Creates direct connections between farmers and bulk buyers.
4. Reduces unnecessary transportation costs.
5. Minimizes food wastage through demand forecasting.
6. Promotes digital awareness among rural farmers.
7. Improves transparency in agricultural trading.
8. Supports sustainable agricultural development.
